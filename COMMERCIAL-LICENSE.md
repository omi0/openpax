Sitli is free to self-host for your own restaurant, restaurant group, or
organization under the Elastic License 2.0 (see `LICENSE`).

The Elastic License 2.0 does **not** allow you to provide Sitli to third parties
as a hosted or managed service (for example, selling restaurant bookings as a
SaaS product built on Sitli), nor to remove or alter license and copyright
notices.

If you want to offer Sitli as a hosted service to others, you need a commercial
license. A commercial license grants hosting rights in exchange for a fee and
requires visible attribution to the Sitli project.

To obtain one, open an issue titled "Commercial license request" in the
project's repository or contact the maintainers at the address listed in the
README.
